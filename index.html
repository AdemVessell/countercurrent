A bounded model

One vertical float coordinate is coupled to linear shallow-water waves in a closed basin. Horizontal dragging is an externally driven guide. This is an energy-derived interaction model, not a calibrated hull simulation, breaking water or a three-dimensional fluid.

The undamped numerical scheme preserves a modified energy. Ordinary energy has timestep error. Damping and your hand add or remove energy. Optical slopes are amplified ×3 for legibility; geometry and measurements keep their original scale. Surface optics are illustrative.
