What’s simulated

The float moves up and down while a shallow-water model carries the waves. Your drag guides it across the basin. Splashing, breaking waves and full hull motion are outside this model.

Damping settles the water; your hand adds energy. The lighting exaggerates surface slopes ×3 so small ripples are easier to see. The geometry and measurements keep their original scale. The model notes explain the equations, numerical checks and approximations.
